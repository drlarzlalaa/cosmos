Welcome

COSMOS keeps three things clearly separated as it investigates the laws of nature: established knowledge (strong, reproducible evidence), open scientific questions (researchable, but unresolved), and speculative hypotheses (not yet supported by evidence). This is currently a Phase 2 build — accounts, the platform shell, and a research library where every entry is tagged with one of those three statuses. The research engines below are scaffolded in the repository but not yet implemented.
