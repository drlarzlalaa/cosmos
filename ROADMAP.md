# COSMOS Roadmap

Built in phases, not all at once. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)
for what each module is for.

| Phase | Components | Objective | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frontend, backend, database, authentication | Establish the platform foundation | **Done** — accounts (register/login), a stdlib API gateway, sqlite storage, and a dashboard/settings shell |
| 2 | Research library, data ingestion, scientific search | Collect and organise reliable knowledge | Not started |
| 3 | Mathematics engine, physics engine, simulations | Test established scientific models | Not started |
| 4 | Hypothesis management, statistical analysis, validation | Build a reliable hypothesis-testing workflow | Not started |
| 5 | AI research agents, literature analysis, model comparison | Automate parts of scientific investigation | Not started |
| 6 | Consciousness, causality, existence, and spiritual research | Expand into interdisciplinary questions | Not started |
| 7 | Universal discovery engine, knowledge graph | Investigate relationships across research domains | Not started |
| 8 | Independent replication, reporting, monitoring | Strengthen reliability and research reproducibility | Not started |

## What "done" means for Phase 1

- `backend/authentication`, `backend/user-management`, `backend/configuration`,
  `backend/api-gateway`, `storage/relational-database`: real, tested code.
- `frontend/login`, `frontend/dashboard`, `frontend/settings`: real pages
  served by the gateway.
- Every other module listed in ARCHITECTURE.md exists as a directory
  with a placeholder `README.md` (generated by `tools/generate_scaffold.py`)
  naming its purpose and the phase it belongs to — not yet implemented.

## Picking up Phase 2+

Each later phase should follow the same pattern as Phase 1: real,
tested, stdlib-first code for the modules in scope, replacing their
placeholder READMEs; everything else stays a scaffold until its own
phase arrives. Phase 2 starts with `data-platform/data-ingestion`,
`backend/search-service`, and `frontend/research-library`.
